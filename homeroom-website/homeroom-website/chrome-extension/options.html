H Homeroom Sync

Every so often this extension opens each site below in a minimized window, using the sign-ins already in Chrome, reads your assignment list, and sends it to your Homeroom. It doesn’t store passwords. It only works while Chrome is open.

1. Connect to your Homeroom In Homeroom open Logins (the key at the top) → Chrome extension → Get my connection code. Paste it here.
2. Sites to check
For best results, sign in to the site, open the page that lists your assignments, click the Homeroom Sync button in the toolbar, and choose “Use this page”. Or paste that page’s address here.
